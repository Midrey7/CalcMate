# Privacy Policy for CalcMate

**Last Updated: September 2026**

Welcome to **CalcMate** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use CalcMate.

By using CalcMate, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use

#### A. Information You Provide to Us
CalcMate is primarily an offline calculation and productivity tool. We do not require you to create an account, register, or provide personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number) to use the core features of the app.
* **Calculation History & Preferences**: Any numbers, formulas, custom settings (theme, precision, haptics), and calculation history are stored **locally on your device** using Android DataStore. We do not transmit, sync, or store your calculations on our servers.

#### B. Third-Party Services and Advertising Data
To support the development and maintenance of CalcMate as a free app, we display third-party advertisements provided by **Google AdMob**.
Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect certain diagnostic and device-related information, including:
* Device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID / GAID)
* Approximate location (derived from IP address)
* App interactions and ad performance data (impressions, clicks)
* Crash logs and performance diagnostics

This data is processed in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy:
👉 [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy)  
👉 [How Google Uses Information from Apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

---

### 2. Advertising and Opt-Out Rights

* **Personalized Advertising**: You can manage or reset your Android Advertising ID at any time through your device settings:  
  `Settings` → `Google` → `Ads` → `Reset advertising ID` or `Delete advertising ID`.
* **Ad Choices**: Depending on your region, you may be presented with a consent form (such as the Google User Messaging Platform) allowing you to choose whether to receive personalized or non-personalized ads.

---

### 3. Permissions Used by the App

CalcMate only requests permissions strictly necessary for its functionality:
* `android.permission.INTERNET`: Required to load advertisements from Google AdMob and check for app updates.
* `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Used by the AdMob SDK to determine whether an active internet connection is present.
* `android.permission.VIBRATE`: Required to provide tactile haptic feedback when you press calculator keys (can be toggled on or off in app Settings).

We **do not** request sensitive permissions such as Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Precise Location, or Storage access.

---

### 4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

CalcMate is a general audience calculator tool and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to a third-party partner via our app, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.

---

### 5. Data Security

The security of your data is important to us. Because your calculation history and preferences remain entirely on your local device, you maintain full control over your data. You can erase all calculation records at any time directly within the app by tapping **Clear All** in the History or Settings screen.
